Privacy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

What this site collects

This website does not use advertising cookies, account registration or a server-side contact database. The motion preference stores one value in your browser so the site can remember whether you paused animation.

Project enquiries

The project form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not submit or store the form contents. Your email provider processes the message only after you choose to send it.

External services

Portfolio preview images currently load from Unsplash. Visiting the site may therefore send standard network information, such as your IP address and browser details, to that image provider. GitHub Pages processes normal hosting logs under GitHub's own privacy terms.

Your choices

You can clear the saved motion preference through your browser's site-data controls. You can also contact Mubbashirul Islam directly to ask a privacy question.
